Thai-English Conversational AI Assistant

  • Python
  • AI/LLM
  • API
  • Natural Language Processing

A bilingual (Thai-English) voice assistant built to remove the barrier of typing. Many AI chat apps assume the user is comfortable typing quickly, which isn't true for everyone — this project targets children, older adults, and anyone with a physical or situational difficulty typing, letting them simply speak their question and hear a spoken answer back.

Key Features

  • Speech-to-Text (STT) — converts spoken audio into text — supporting both Thai and English, via the Google Web Speech API.
  • The transcribed question is sent to Google's Gemini large language model, and the reply is both shown as text and read aloud using gTTS (Google Text-to-Speech, a library that converts written text back into spoken audio).
  • A language toggle in the interface lets the user pick Thai or English before starting a conversation.
  • Common questions (like asking the time or the assistant's name) have pre-written, instant responses rather than being sent to the AI model.
  • If the assistant can't understand what was said, it shows a clear message and lets the user simply try again.

Choosing an AI Model

Three AI models were compared before settling on one — Google's Gemini, OpenAI's GPT, and OpenAI's Whisper — weighing accuracy, language support, response speed, and whether each could run offline.

  • Whisper — the most accurate, especially for English, but takes longer to process each request.
  • Gemini — the fastest of the three, with strong multi-language support, at a small cost to raw accuracy.
  • GPT — a middle ground between speed and accuracy.

Gemini was chosen because a conversational assistant needs to feel responsive above all else.

Evaluation Criteria

The finished assistant was evaluated against five criteria:

  • How relevant and accurate Gemini's answers were to what was actually asked.
  • How well the speech-to-text step performed at correctly transcribing spoken Thai and English.
  • How long the full round-trip took, from speaking to hearing a reply.
  • Whether replies consistently came back in the language the user had selected, and how stable the app was over repeated use.
  • How gracefully the app handled unclear or invalid audio input, rather than crashing or freezing.

Future Work

  • Give the chatbot the ability to learn and adapt in real time from conversations.
  • Add a more consistent personality and emotional tone to responses.
  • Adapt the assistant into an intelligent customer-service tool capable of handling more complex problems.
  • Extend it into a multi-modal AI that can also understand images, audio, and video, not just text.
