4WD Mecanum Robot for Ball Color Sorting

  • Arduino
  • Mecanum Wheels
  • RGB Color Sensor
  • Servo Motor
  • HC-06 Bluetooth
  • Robotics

A Mechatronics Engineering project focused on the design and development of a 4WD Mecanum-wheel robot for collecting and sorting balls by color. The robot is controlled by a smartphone through HC-06 Bluetooth communication and uses servo mechanisms and an RGB color sensor to collect, detect, and sort balls into different compartments.

How It Works

  • The robot is controlled from a smartphone through the HC-06 Bluetooth module.
  • Four gear motors drive the Mecanum wheels, allowing forward, backward, lateral, and rotational movement.
  • A servo-controlled mechanism collects and transfers the ball into the sorting system.
  • An RGB color sensor detects the color of the ball.
  • A servo mechanism rotates the ball toward the corresponding color compartment.

Mecanum Wheel System

Mecanum wheels use rollers arranged at approximately 45° to provide multi-directional movement. By controlling the rotation of all four wheels in different combinations, the robot can move forward, backward, sideways, and rotate around its own axis without changing its orientation.

Color Detection & Sorting

The RGB color sensor is used to distinguish different ball colors based on red, green, and blue light components. After detecting the ball color, the servo-controlled sorting mechanism moves the ball to its corresponding compartment.

The system was designed to collect four different colors of balls, with five balls for each color, within a target time of three minutes.

My Contribution

  • Designed and assembled mechanical structures for ball collection and sorting.
  • Integrated Mecanum wheels, gear motors, servo motors, and the RGB color sensor.
  • Developed the control system for robot movement and ball collection.
  • Integrated HC-06 Bluetooth communication for smartphone control.
  • Tested sensor accuracy, motor operation, servo movement, and mechanical performance.

Challenges & Solutions

  • Improved color detection accuracy by adjusting the sensor position closer to the ball.
  • Modified the power system to provide sufficient current for motor operation.
  • Replaced an unsuitable servo motor and reduced the mechanical load to improve servo performance.

What I Learned

This project strengthened my practical understanding of robotics, Mecanum-wheel movement, RGB sensor integration, servo control, mechanical design, Bluetooth communication, and hardware troubleshooting.
