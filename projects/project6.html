Servo-Controlled 4WD Mecanum Robot for Ball Retrieval

  • Arduino
  • Mecanum Wheels
  • Servo Motor
  • RGB Color Sensor
  • Bluetooth
  • Robotics

Project Overview

Designed and developed a 4WD Mecanum-wheel robot for collecting and sorting colored balls. The robot is controlled via a smartphone through Bluetooth communication and uses Mecanum wheels for forward, backward, lateral, and rotational movement. Servo mechanisms are used to collect and transfer balls, while an RGB color sensor detects ball colors and controls the sorting process.

Key Features

  • 4WD Mecanum-wheel drive for omnidirectional movement
  • Smartphone control through HC-06 Bluetooth
  • Servo-controlled ball retrieval mechanism
  • RGB color sensing for ball identification
  • Servo mechanism for sorting balls into different compartments
  • Designed to collect 4 colors of balls, 5 balls per color, within a 3-minute target
